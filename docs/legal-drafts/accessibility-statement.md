## Improvements in the remediation release

Recent engineering changes include a keyboard-managed age-confirmation dialog, access to legal/privacy pages before age confirmation, a main-content landmark and skip link, visible focus styles, labeled checkout fields and associated errors, keyboard-accessible privacy choices, improved account/review controls and reduced-motion styling.

These changes have focused automated and synthetic browser coverage. They do not establish complete compatibility with every screen reader, browser, device or third-party checkout/sign-in component.

## Ongoing work and limitations

We continue to review the complete purchase flow, zoom/reflow, contrast, screen-reader behavior, dynamic updates and embedded payment/sign-in components. Third-party tools and externally linked sites may have separate limitations. A third-party dependency is not a reason to ignore a reported barrier; contact us so we can investigate and discuss alternatives.
