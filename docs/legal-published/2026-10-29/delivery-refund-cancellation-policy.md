# Delivery, Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026
Merchant: Balport Liquors

## Service availability and charges

Pickup and delivery availability depend on the products, destination, stock, operating hours and applicable restrictions. The checkout shows the available methods and approved delivery ZIP codes. A supported ZIP is an eligibility check, not a guarantee that every address or requested delivery time can be served.

The delivery fee shown at checkout comes from the store's configured fee schedule. Taxes, store charges, discounts and any selected tip are included in the total shown before payment. Pickup does not incur a delivery fee or driver tip in the website checkout. No free-delivery threshold is promised unless a specific approved promotion says so.

## Submission, acceptance and fulfillment

A payment confirmation is not a dispatch notice. Online orders initially await staff acceptance. Staff arrange fulfillment; the website does not automatically guarantee a DoorDash or Uber driver booking. When pickup is selected, use the confirmed store location and wait for the appropriate readiness instructions before traveling.

Displayed store location: **4521 West Coast Hwy, Newport Beach, CA 92663**. 

Test-mode orders are demonstrations rather than real paid orders. Live checkout and actual restricted-product fulfillment require separate approval and operational procedures.

## Age, identification and safe handoff

Alcohol recipients must be 21 or older, be present and provide valid identification when required. Online age acknowledgment is not a substitute for the handoff check. Alcohol cannot be supplied to minors or obviously intoxicated persons. Do not request an unattended alcohol drop-off.

If required verification cannot be completed, the order must not be handed over. 

## Cancelling or changing an order

While an order remains pending and has not been accepted, the account order screen may offer cancellation. The store may also reject an order that cannot be fulfilled. For cancellations or rejections completed through the website, we attempt a full refund of the website order total to the original payment.

After acceptance, contact us promptly. Online customer cancellation is no longer offered automatically. We will review the actual fulfillment and payment state before confirming what can be changed or cancelled.

## Failed delivery and returned goods

If the recipient is unavailable, identification cannot be verified, an address cannot be served or a delivery otherwise fails, contact the store. Staff record the known facts and review the order, goods and payment status. A provider's returned status is not proof of a particular reason or automatic approval of a refund.

Recording a failed delivery or returned order does not automatically issue a partial refund or apply a failed-delivery or return fee. Contact the store for review of the appropriate remedy. A return is recorded only after staff confirms physical receipt. The appropriate remedy requires review of the order and returned goods.

## Missing, damaged, incorrect or unavailable items

Contact us promptly if items are missing, damaged or different from your order, or if an item becomes unavailable. Tell us the order number and what happened. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to investigate. Do not ship or bring back alcohol until the store gives appropriate instructions.

## Refund processing and payment recovery

Refunds are processed against the original Stripe payment where supported. A requested or pending processor refund is not a completed refund; bank or payment-method posting can take additional time. We do not promise a specific posting period without confirming it for the relevant payment method.

If a payment succeeds but an order cannot be completed, the website attempts to recover the original checkout or reconcile a refund. Some interrupted, disputed or partially refunded payments require staff review. Do not pay again just because confirmation is delayed. Keep the original reference and contact us if recovery remains pending.

We use the original payment and refund references to reduce duplicate requests. It does not authorize the store to keep a customer's money without a lawful basis. 

## Your rights and policy changes

Nothing in this policy limits rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be waived. Approved changes will have a new effective date and any notice required by law. Earlier orders remain subject to the applicable terms and mandatory consumer protections.
