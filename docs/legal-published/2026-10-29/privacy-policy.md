## Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, we collect:

| Category | Examples and source | Main purposes |
|---|---|---|
| Account and contact information | Name, email, phone, account credentials and profile details you provide | Create and protect accounts, communicate and support orders |
| Order and transaction information | Items, quantities, totals, discounts, payment references, fulfillment choice and order/refund status | Process and reconcile purchases, manage inventory and resolve problems |
| Delivery or pickup information | Address, city, state, ZIP, country and pickup instructions you provide | Check service availability and arrange fulfillment |
| Sign-in information | Account identity and verification information supplied through Google when you choose Google sign-in | Authenticate and manage your account |
| Customer activity and content | Wishlist, recent products, product reviews, ratings and any images or communications you submit | Provide requested features, publish reviews and respond to support requests |
| Technical and security information | Browser requests, IP addresses and security/session records generated when you visit or sign in | Operate, troubleshoot and protect the service |
| Optional website analytics | Pages or products viewed, a browser identifier, coarse device/browser information and referring site origin | Understand site use when optional analytics is enabled |
| Age and fulfillment acknowledgments | Age acknowledgment and, where recorded, staff confirmation of required handoff checks | Apply restricted-product controls and review fulfillment |

Passwords are hashed. The website's payment flow uses Stripe payment components and stores payment references rather than complete card numbers or card security codes. Your payment information is submitted to Stripe through its components. 

The website's age confirmation is a self-attestation. It is not an identity-document verification service. The current website does not provide an identity-document upload flow. Identification checks at pickup or delivery are handled separately from the website acknowledgment.

## How information is used

We use information to provide requested accounts and shopping features; calculate and process orders; check delivery eligibility; arrange pickup or delivery; send verification and order communications; investigate payment, stock, delivery or refund issues; protect against fraud and unauthorized access; respond to privacy and support requests; and meet applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

Optional analytics is used only when enabled through the website's privacy controls. Browser-local cart and preference records may remain on your device until you remove them or the application clears them.

## Service providers and other disclosures

Information may be disclosed as needed to payment processors, sign-in providers, email providers, delivery providers, infrastructure/security providers and services used to store product or user-submitted images. For example, a delivery may require the recipient's name, phone number, address and relevant order details. Staff may arrange delivery manually; use of the website does not mean DoorDash or another provider has automatically accepted an order.

We may also disclose relevant information when necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or complete an applicable business transfer, subject to required protections.

Public product reviews may display your review, rating and account name/profile information. Do not include information you do not want other visitors to see. 

## Cookies, browser storage and privacy choices

Essential cookies and browser storage support authentication, security, your cart and saved preferences. Optional store analytics remains off until you select **Accept All**. **Essential Only** keeps optional analytics off. You can reopen **Privacy choices** in the footer and change your decision.

This website does not use Google Analytics. Optional store analytics is separate from Google sign-in and payment services. Google sign-in, payment components and other services you choose to use may make their own service-related requests.

When the browser supplies Global Privacy Control, the website disables optional store analytics even if you previously opted in. For questions about information disclosures or to submit an opt-out request, use [Privacy requests](/privacy-requests) or contact us.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information for the purposes described here and for applicable payment reconciliation, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, dispute and legal requirements. Retention depends on the type of record and any applicable hold.

Optional store analytics records are scheduled for removal after approximately 90 days. Removal may not be immediate; legacy records, logs, vendors and backups require separate review. Encrypted backups and related records are not immediately erased when a privacy request is submitted.

## Privacy requests and California rights

Where applicable law provides them, you may have rights to know/access personal information, receive a portable copy, request correction or deletion, opt out of sale/sharing, limit certain uses of sensitive information, and receive service without unlawful discrimination for exercising rights. Rights and exceptions depend on the applicable law and circumstances.

Use [Privacy requests](/privacy-requests) on the website or contact us using the details above. Signed-in customers can submit and track requests and download available account information. You may contact us without creating an account. The account download does not automatically include records held only by vendors, support channels, retained backups or browser-only/anonymous records that cannot reliably be linked to the account.

We may need information reasonably necessary to verify a request and protect against unauthorized access or deletion. Do not send passwords or identity documents through ordinary email. We will explain a suitable verification method when needed. 

A deletion request is reviewed before records are erased. Financial obligations, legal holds and other permitted exceptions may require retaining some information. We will explain the response and applicable limitations rather than treating submission as completed deletion.

## Age restrictions and security

The site is intended for adults aged 21 or older. Do not use restricted-product services if you do not meet the required age and eligibility conditions. If you believe a child provided information, contact us so we can review and address it. 

We use access controls and other measures designed to protect information. No website or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Keep your account credentials private and report suspected unauthorized account use promptly.

## Changes

We will update this policy when approved practices or applicable requirements change, identify the effective date, and provide any notice or choices required by law. Previously collected information remains subject to applicable requirements concerning material changes.
